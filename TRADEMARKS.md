- Refer to the project as GlideFrame when discussing, reviewing, documenting, or linking to the official project.
- Say that your work is based on GlideFrame or compatible with GlideFrame.
- Use the GlideFrame name in package metadata, documentation, or dependency references when it is clear that you are referring to the upstream project.

## You May Not

- Present a fork, modified build, service, website, or distribution as the official GlideFrame project.

- Remove or alter attribution in a way that misrepresents the origin of the project.

## Forks and Derivatives

Forks and derivative projects should use a distinct name and visual identity. A clear phrase such as "based on GlideFrame" is welcome when it does not imply official endorsement.
